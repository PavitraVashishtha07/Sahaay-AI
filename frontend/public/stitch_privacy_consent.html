Interactive Architecture & Consent Lifecycle Specification

Consent Revocation Flow (3B) & Cross-Screen Blocked Fallback (3C)

In strict compliance with DPDP Act 2023 and RBI Account Aggregator guidelines, consent revocation must be frictionless, non-punitive, and fully transparent. Below are the canonical interaction panels.

State 3B • Revoke Confirmation Dialog (Modal Overlay)
Trigger: Click "Stop sharing"

क्या आप वाकई डेटा रोकना चाहते हैं?
Are you sure?

यदि आप डेटा साझा करना रोकते हैं, तो जब तक आप इसे दोबारा चालू नहीं करते, सहाय आपको व्यक्तिगत वित्तीय सुझाव नहीं दे पाएगा और न ही आपके खाते से जुड़े सवालों के सीधे उत्तर दे पाएगा।

"If you stop sharing, Sahaay won't be able to give you personalized suggestions or answer questions about your account until you turn it back on. You can turn it back on anytime."

🛡️ Non-Punitive Modal Design Principles:

  • Zero Danger Cues: Strictly forbidden from using red danger colors, alert exclamation triangles, or loss aversion dark patterns. Revoking is a fundamental privacy right.
  • In-Place Status Mutation: Confirmation triggers POST /consent/revoke, then mutates the Status Card to a calm grey "Inactive / निष्क्रिय" state without forcing a navigation out.

When /arbitrate or /data receives a revoked status, frontend interceptors swap the recommendation card with this peaceful state. Under no circumstances does the product display raw HTTP errors, broken wireframes, or warning toasts.
